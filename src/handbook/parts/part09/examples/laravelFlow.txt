A form in Laravel (Blade)

1. The browser shows a <form> rendered by Blade.
2. The user clicks Submit. The BROWSER sends a POST and leaves the page.
3. Laravel validates the request.
4a. Invalid: Laravel redirects back. The page reloads completely.
    old('customer') refills the fields, $errors shows the messages.
4b. Valid: Laravel saves the order and redirects (e.g. to orders.show).
    The page reloads again with a flash message.
